Synthetic, team-created reference data for UniFlow's supported programmes
(no real institutional data). UniFlow simulates two colleges:

College of Computing and Information Sciences (CoCIS):
- BSSE — BSc Software Engineering, 4 years
- BSCS — BSc Computer Science, 3 years
- BIST — BSc Information Systems Technology, 3 years

College of Engineering, Art and Design (CEAD):
- BCVE — BSc Civil Engineering, 4 years
- BELE — BSc Electrical Engineering, 4 years
- BQSV — BSc Quantity Surveying, 4 years
- BMCE — BSc Mechanical Engineering, 4 years

Programme codes for CEAD are team-assigned placeholders for the prototype,
not official institutional codes.

This document lists which programmes exist and how many years each takes.
It deliberately does not restate which specific year of which programme
supports Evening study — that is a separate, approved business rule with
its own provenance, kept in KB-R02-COMBINATIONS so the two facts ("what
programmes exist" vs. "which of them run in the evening") have exactly one
source each rather than two documents that could drift out of sync.
