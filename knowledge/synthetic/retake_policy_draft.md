This is an early draft circulated informally while scoping a future
retake-management workflow. It has not been reviewed or ratified, and it
is NOT part of the approved UniFlow rule pack.

Draft proposal (unapproved): a student may retake a failed course up to 2
times, and a retake registration would follow the same course-load rule
(R-04) as an ordinary registration.

Per the Week 1 Charter scope exclusions, retake management is deferred to
a later requirements iteration. Any answer that treats the numbers above
as an active rule is wrong — they are a proposal, not a decision. The
correct response to a question about retakes is "insufficient_context",
optionally noting that an unreviewed draft exists.
