The "applicable semester" referenced in US-05 (semester enrollment) follows
an academic calendar maintained by the registrar's office. Specific
semester start and end dates are not enumerated in this knowledge base and
are outside UniFlow's simulated scope — UniFlow checks enrollment status
and eligibility, not calendar dates. Do not invent specific semester dates
when answering a question about enrollment timing.
