Informal memo floated by a programme coordinator, suggesting that a
student missing a prerequisite could be allowed to register "in
exceptional cases, with coordinator sign-off." This has not been adopted
into the approved rule pack and does not modify R-07.

Decision authority over any such exception rests with human academic
staff, never with the QA Agent, and no waiver mechanism exists in the
current approved requirements (US-07). Treat this memo as background only
— it must not be presented as an approved exception process.
