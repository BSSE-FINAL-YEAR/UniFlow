For the UniFlow prototype, Evening (EVE) study is available only to:

- BSc Software Engineering (BSSE) — Year 4 only
- BSc Computer Science (BSCS) — Year 3 only

BSc Information Systems Technology (BIST) and all College of Engineering,
Art and Design (CEAD) programmes — BSc Civil Engineering, BSc Electrical
Engineering, BSc Quantity Surveying, BSc Mechanical Engineering — are Day
(PS) only, in every year.

Day (PS) study is available in every year of every programme. This table
is the complete, approved answer to "which programme/year/mode
combinations are supported" referenced by R-02 and US-04 — it is a
decision made for the UniFlow prototype's scope, not part of the
originally supplied documentation, which is why it is tagged as a UniFlow
Business Rule rather than source-derived.
