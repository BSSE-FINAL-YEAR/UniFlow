CEAD programmes are four-year programmes: BSc Civil Engineering, BSc
Electrical Engineering, BSc Quantity Surveying and BSc Mechanical
Engineering. CoCIS includes BSc Software Engineering (4 years), BSc
Computer Science (3 years) and BSc Information Systems Technology
(3 years). See KB-R02-COMBINATIONS for which of these run in Day vs.
Evening mode.
