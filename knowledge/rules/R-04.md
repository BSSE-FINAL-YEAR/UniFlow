provenance: UniFlow Business Rule
status: approved
workflow: course_registration
rule_id: R-04
---
Maximum course load is 6 units in Year 1, 6 units in Year 2, 5 units in
Year 3 and 4 units in Year 4. A registration that exceeds the applicable
limit for the student's year is rejected.
