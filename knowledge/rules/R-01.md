title: R-01 Student identity
provenance: source-derived
status: approved
workflow: student_registration
rule_id: R-01
---
Registration numbers preserve the source format, e.g. 23/U/05288/PS or
23/U/05288/EVE. The student number is derived from the registration number
(e.g. 2305288) and is unique regardless of the PS/EVE suffix.
