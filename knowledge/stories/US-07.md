As a student, I want prerequisite requirements checked so that I cannot
register for a course without satisfying its prerequisites.

Acceptance criteria:
- A student who has satisfied all prerequisites can register.
- A student missing a required prerequisite is rejected.
- The unmet prerequisite is identifiable in the validation result.
