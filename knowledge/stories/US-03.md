As an administrator, I want duplicate derived student numbers rejected so
that PS and EVE do not create two records for the same student number.

Acceptance criteria:
- A second record with an existing derived student number is rejected.
- The PS/EVE suffix does not make the derived student number unique.
- The system reports a clear validation error.
