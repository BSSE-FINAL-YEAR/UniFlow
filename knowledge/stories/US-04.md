As an administrator, I want study mode restricted to supported
programme/year combinations so that students cannot be registered into an
unavailable mode.

Acceptance criteria:
- Only defined PS/EVE combinations are accepted.
- An unsupported programme/year/mode combination is rejected.
- The reason for rejection is recorded or displayed.

See R-02: the set of "defined" combinations is asserted but not enumerated
anywhere in this knowledge base.
