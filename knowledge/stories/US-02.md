As an administrator, I want student email addresses validated so that
student records use the expected university email format.

Acceptance criteria:
- A valid university student email is accepted.
- An invalid format is rejected.
- The email is stored with the student record after successful validation.

This story requires "the expected university email format" to be enforced,
but no rule in this knowledge base defines that format (no domain, no
pattern). That is a genuine gap in the approved requirements, not an
oversight to be filled in from outside knowledge.
