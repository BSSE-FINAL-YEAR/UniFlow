As a student, I want duplicate course registrations prevented so that the
same course cannot be registered twice in one applicable period.

Acceptance criteria:
- A course can be registered only once for the applicable semester/period.
- A duplicate request is rejected.
- The existing registration is not duplicated or corrupted.
