As a student, I want to enroll for a semester so that I can proceed to
course registration.

Acceptance criteria:
- A valid student can enroll for the applicable semester.
- Required prerequisite conditions for enrollment are checked.
- An invalid/ineligible enrollment request is rejected.
- Enrollment status is stored and available to later course-registration
  checks.
