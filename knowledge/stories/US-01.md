As an academic/registration administrator, I want to register a student so
that a valid student record exists in UniFlow.

Acceptance criteria:
- A valid registration number is accepted and preserved.
- A student number is derived from the registration number.
- The derived student number is unique regardless of PS/EVE.
- Required student and programme fields are validated before registration
  is completed.
