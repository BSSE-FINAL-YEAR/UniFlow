As a student, I want to register for an eligible course so that my
semester registration reflects my approved study plan.

Acceptance criteria:
- The course belongs to the student's programme or is otherwise explicitly
  eligible.
- The course is available for the semester/year.
- Required enrollment and eligibility checks pass before registration.
