As a student, I want my course load limited according to year so that I
cannot exceed the UniFlow maximum.

Acceptance criteria:
- Year 1 maximum is 6 units.
- Year 2 maximum is 6 units.
- Year 3 maximum is 5 units.
- Year 4 maximum is 4 units.
- A registration that exceeds the applicable limit is rejected.

See R-04 for the source rule these limits are drawn from.
