As an administrator, I want a timetable generated within UniFlow
constraints so that teaching sessions do not create prohibited conflicts.

Acceptance criteria:
- Day sessions fall within 08:00-16:00.
- Evening sessions fall within 16:30-20:30.
- Each course is taught at least once and at most twice per week.
- Student, lecturer and room conflicts are detected/prevented.
- The generated timetable contains identifiable course, time and room
  information.

See R-05 and R-06 for the source rules these constraints are drawn from.
